66. FIRST-DIVERGENCE RULE
======================================================================

For every mismatch, do not immediately change a formula.

First determine:

    What is the expected input?
    What is Mini-STAAD actually using?
    Where do the values first diverge?
    What engineering equation should govern that stage?
    Is the implementation mathematically correct?
    Is the reference behavior compatible with it?

Then fix the earliest incorrect stage.
